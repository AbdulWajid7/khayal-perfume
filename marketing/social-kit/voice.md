## What we sound like

- Warm, quietly confident, elegant but simple. A friend who knows perfume, not an ad.
- Short sentences. Concrete smells people know: Karachi rain, chai, motia, mehndi night,
  a freshly ironed kurta, the first cold morning.
- Roman Urdu where it is natural, never forced: khushboo, shaadi, mehndi, dawat, yaar,
  bas, ek spray. One or two words per post, not whole sentences, unless the post is
  written for an Urdu audience.
- Emoji: rarely, one at most, never as bullets.
- To camera (when the founder speaks): calm, a little dry, about 150 words a minute.

**Words we use:** extrait, 38% perfume oil, long-lasting, tester, cash on delivery, made in
Karachi, notes, trail, on skin, collar, wrist.

**Words we never use:** dupe, first copy, replica, clone, inspired by, impression, "smells
exactly like", any designer brand or perfume name, eau de parfum/EDP (we are extrait),
"best in Pakistan", luxury overload (exquisite, opulent, symphony, olfactory journey,
elevate), cures/therapy/aphrodisiac/pheromones, "stop scrolling", "tag someone".

## Our positions (where the good posts come from)

1. Concentration matters more than the label. 38% oil is why it lasts through a function.
2. You should smell a perfume before paying for 50 ml. That is why every order has a tester.
3. Karachi heat kills weak perfume. Wear less, on skin, and pick the right scent for the hour.
4. A scent is a memory (khayal). People remember how you smelled at their wedding.
5. A good perfume from Karachi does not need to pretend to be foreign.
