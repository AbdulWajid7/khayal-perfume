## What it means for KHAYAL

1. **Price:** our 50 ml extrait will be compared with THE NOTE. at about Rs 1,700 and
   impression brands below that. A higher price needs visible reasons in every post:
   38% oil, the free tester, longevity shown on camera, cash on delivery, made in Karachi.
2. **Offer parity:** free delivery and a free sample/tester are now normal. Our tester is
   table stakes; make it special (a different scent so they discover a second favourite).
3. **Reviews win:** THE NOTE. shows 532. We need real reviews fast: the WhatsApp review
   template after delivery, the QR on the tester card, and the Terrace weekend.
4. **SEO pattern:** every competitor ranks with "top 10 perfume brands in Pakistan" and
   "long lasting perfume for men/women" guides. We do not name other brands publicly, so we
   win with guides (the daily journal) and by getting **listed on other people's lists**
   (see backlinks.md).
5. **Content gap:** most local brands post product photos and sale banners. Few show the
   scent: ingredients, longevity tests, real reactions, Karachi moments. That is our lane.

## Hooks that work in fragrance right now (global PerfumeTok, 2026)

- Compliment stories ("three people asked what I'm wearing") with real reactions.
- "This smells like..." sensory comparisons (we use real smells: rain, chai, motia; never
  "smells like [designer]").
- Vibe and identity lists ("scents that smell expensive", "office scents that won't
  choke the meeting room").
- Longevity tests on camera (morning to night).
- Micro-creators doing casual, friend-style recommendations at launch.
- Scarcity done honestly (limited Terrace testers, launch-day batch), never fake.
- Banned for us: the "dupe for X" hook, even though it is the biggest one on TikTok.
