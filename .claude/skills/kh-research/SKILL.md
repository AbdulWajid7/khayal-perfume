# kh-research

No KHAYAL content is written blind. Every piece starts from a short brief built
on evidence. 10-15 minutes per piece, 30-45 minutes for a weekly plan.

## Read first

- `marketing/social-kit/competitors.md` (who does what, prices, offers)
- `marketing/social-kit/swipe.md` (what is working, from `kh-trends`)
- `marketing/social-kit/log.md` (what we already posted; do not repeat an angle
  within 3 weeks)
- `marketing/social-kit/voice.md` (facts, rules, dates)

## Research (use the tools this session has)

1. **Search demand**: web search for the topic in Pakistan ("long lasting
   perfume for men Pakistan", "wedding perfume"); Google autocomplete and
   "People also ask" via search results; OpenSEO Search Console data
   (`get_search_console_performance`, free) for what already brings us traffic.
   Keyword-volume tools cost OpenSEO credits: ask the owner first.
2. **Competitors on this topic**: what THE NOTE., Sesky, J., WB by Hemani and
   others posted about it recently (their sites, blogs, public pages). Note the
   hook, format, offer and what is missing.
3. **Platform trends**: format and hook patterns working now (`swipe.md`, web
   search for PerfumeTok/Reels trends this month), commercial-library audio
   only, seasonal moments (weddings, weather, Eid, payday, gifting dates).
4. **Ads in the niche** (optional, needs the owner's OK to use AdWhispr quota):
   `search_ads`/`get_brand_ads` for active perfume ads in Pakistan; long-running
   ads are the proven ones.

Browsing rules: public pages only, human speed, never log in as the owner,
never scrape at volume.

## Output: the brief (keep it to one screen)

```
BRIEF  ·  SILK ROYALE wedding reel  ·  12 Oct
Search:      "wedding perfume for women" rising before Nov shaadi season
Competitors: THE NOTE. posts product flat-lays + discounts; nobody shows a
             perfume lasting through a full function
Working now: Hours Test and Occasion Rescue hooks (swipe.md), 12-20 s
Gap/angle:   show 6 pm mehndi -> midnight, real dupatta, real clock
Hooks to try: #29 Hours Test, #31 Occasion Rescue, #27 Smell It Through The Screen
Keywords:    wedding perfume Pakistan, long lasting perfume for women
Avoid:       naming brands, "dupe", fake compliments
```

Then hand the brief to the skill that makes the piece. Add any new competitor
fact to `competitors.md` with the date and source.
