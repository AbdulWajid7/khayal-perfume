# kh-engage

Twenty good comments a day on the right accounts bring more Karachi followers
than any hashtag. A comment is a tiny post: it has to be worth reading.

## Who to engage with (owner approves the list, kept in voice.md later)

- Karachi venues and cafes (@theterracecafe.pk first), bridal and formal-wear
  designers, wedding photographers and planners, makeup artists.
- Karachi and Lahore lifestyle creators with 5k-100k followers.
- Fragrance lovers and reviewers in Pakistan (comment as a fellow perfume
  person, never pitch under their posts).
- Our own followers who post (when tagged).

## Comment types (pick by the post)

| Post | Comment |
|---|---|
| Event, wedding, outfit | A specific compliment on one detail + a scent pairing in a few words ("that ivory gharara deserves something warm, saffron and vanilla") without naming our product unless asked. |
| A question or poll | Actually answer it. |
| A tip or opinion | Add one fact or a small disagreement, politely. |
| Fragrance review (other brands) | Talk about the notes or the experience. Never mention their brand negatively, never plug ours. |
| A partner's post | Thank them by name, mention the date or the thing together. |
| Sad or serious news | Skip, or a short human line. Never a pitch. |

## Rules

- 8-25 words. No emoji strings, no "🔥🔥🔥", no "check our page", no links.
- Never the same comment twice. Never on posts older than 3 days.
- Human speed: the owner posts these by hand from the app. No bots, no
  automated liking, following or commenting (that gets accounts blocked).
- Print a list: account · post (one line) · comment. The owner posts them.
