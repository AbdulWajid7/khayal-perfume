# kh-dm

DMs are where orders happen. Short, human, one clear next step.

## Before writing

Read `marketing/social-kit/voice.md`. Know what is confirmed (prices, tester,
delivery) and what is not.

## Inbound (customers)

- **"Price?"**: the confirmed price and size, the free tester, cash on delivery,
  then "Which one caught your eye? I'll tell you how it wears." If prices are
  not confirmed yet: "Launching Tue 20 Oct, I'll message you the moment prices
  are live" and note the handle for the owner.
- **"Which one should I get?"**: two questions max (for whom, for what), then a
  pick with one line on why, and the scent finder link.
- **Order**: name, city, scent; then hand to WhatsApp (+92 320 2704617) where the
  bot and team take orders. Never collect card or bank details in DMs.

## The keyword auto-reply

Instagram allows keyword auto-replies only through its own tools or approved
partners, and only after someone comments or messages first. Text for
"TESTER": one line of thanks, the scent finder link, "reply here if you want
help choosing". Setting it up is the owner's job in the Meta app.

## Outbound pitches (the owner sends them)

Only to businesses and creators who fit, one at a time, never bulk.
- **Cafes / venues** (like The Terrace Cafe): what we bring (a free try-on
  table, testers for guests), what we ask (a table, a weekend, a tag), dates.
- **Wedding planners / bridal boutiques**: testers for brides, a small
  commission or gift set (only if the owner set one).
- **Creators (Karachi lifestyle, 5k-100k followers)**: a gifted bottle, no
  script, honest review, #gifted or paid partnership label required. Never ask
  them to hide that it was gifted.

Structure: their name, one specific thing you liked of theirs, the offer in one
line, the ask in one line. Under 80 words.

## Follow-ups

Two at most: one after 3 days ("just floating this back up"), one after a week
with something new (a photo, a date). Then stop.

## Rules

`brand.py` on every message. No designer names, no copy words, no invented
numbers. Never send anything yourself: the owner sends, or approves each one.
